What is New Brief RSS?

I'm building focused reading workspace where you can follow the outlets and information you want, without getting stuck in an algorithm or lost digging through newsletters in your inbox. Now, your daily news briefing can be exactly the sources you trust, without any ads, junk or clickbait.

  • Subscribe once and refresh from one place.
  • Read stories in chronological order without algorithmic ranking.
  • Track what you've read so you can pick up where you left off or revisit stories later.
