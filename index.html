What is New Brief RSS?

News Brief RSS is, most simply, a focused reading workspace where you can follow the outlets and information you want. The perk is you can do this without getting stuck in an algorithm or lost in your email inbox digging through newsletters. Now, your daily news briefing can be exactly the sources you trust, without any ads, junk or clickbait.

    With News Brief RSS, you can:

  • Subscribe once and refresh all news outlets from one place.
  • Read news stories in chronological order.
  • Log what you've read so far to pick up where you left off or revisit stories later.

How does it work?

News Brief RSS is a web-based RSS reader, short for Really Simple Syndication. RSS is a standard format for delivering regularly changing web content; most news website, blogs and podcasts offer RSS feeds for people to subscribe and follow content published digitally.

  • With News Brief RSS, you can add any RSS or Atom feed to your account, and then refresh all of your feeds to see the latest stories in one place.
  • News Brief RSS fetches the latest stories from your subscribed feeds and displays them in a clean, chronological list.
  • You can mark stories as read, and filter your reading list to show only unread stories.

Why use us?

News Brief RSS is different from other RSS readers because it is designed to be simple and focused on the reading experience. With a nostalgic feel and design, we take your news reading back a few decades without ads, banners or annoying pop-up videos. News Brief RSS collects no data on you, has no algorithmic bias and best of all has no AI chatbot bothering you. Just you, your feeds and your reading list.
