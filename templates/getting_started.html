Getting Started

New here? Let's get you your account set up and add your first stories.

Quick setup in 4 steps

  1. Create an account. {% if g.user %} You're already signed in as {{ g.user.username }}. {% else %} Go to Sign up to create your account. {% endif %}
  2. Add your first feed. Visit Feeds and paste an RSS/Atom URL.
  3. Refresh your feeds. Use the Refresh all feeds button to fetch the newest stories.
  4. Read and organize. Open stories, then mark them read to keep your reading list clean.

What is an RSS feed?

  • An RSS feed is a type of web feed that allows users to access updates to online content in a standardized, computer-readable format.
  • Unlike reading a website or a newsletter, an RSS feed delivers updates directly to your feed reader.
  • This means it's a little messier and less curated, but there's no human bias in what stories you're seeing, just a raw dump.

Where should I look for RSS feeds?

    There are a variety of sites that aggregate RSS Feeds to make it easy to import!

  • Feeder
  • RSS Lookup
  • For a "starter pack" of RSS feeds, check out YouNeedFeeds.com
  • If you're looking to explore new blogs or topics, consider checking out ooh.directory

I don't know where to start!

    For a New Yorker, I recommend starting with the following feeds:

  • New York Times NY/Regional
  • The City Reporter
  • Hell Gate
  • Gothamist
  • One note: adding a feed won't bypass paywalls; this app just organizes story links.
