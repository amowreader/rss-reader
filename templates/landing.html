What is New Brief RSS?

I'm building a focused reading workspace where you can follow the outlets and information you want, without getting stuck in an algorithm or lost in your inbox digging through newsletters. Now, your daily news briefing can be exactly the sources you trust, without any ads, junk or clickbait.

  • Subscribe once and refresh all news outlets from one place.
  • Read news stories in chronological order without algorithmic ranking.
  • Log what you've read so far to pick up where you left off or revisit stories later.
